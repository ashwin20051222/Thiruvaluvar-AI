Epporul Yaaryaarvaaik Kaetpinum Apporul / Meipporul Kaanba Dharivu.

Though things diverse from divers sages' lips we learn, 'tis wisdom's part in each the true thing to discern.

Commentary

Though things diverse from divers sages' lips we learn, 'tis wisdom's part in each the true thing to discern.

Daily Wisdom Reflection Discernment

True wisdom lies not in who speaks, but in discerning the enduring truth behind every claim.
